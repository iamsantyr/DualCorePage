About Us

We are a digital-native company founded in 2025 in Bogotá, Colombia, specializing in machine learning and agentic workflows to help organizations automate decisions and build intelligent products.

Our Story

We are a digital-native company founded in 2025 in Bogotá, Colombia, where engineering, design and innovation unite to create ML-powered and agentic workflow solutions. Founded by Sergio López and Santiago Rodríguez, we bring together software engineering expertise and business development to deliver innovative AI solutions that solve real-world problems with elegant, easy-to-understand implementations.

Our Mission

Design and develop solutions that combine machine learning models, modern cloud architectures and intelligent agents capable of orchestrating complex tasks, integrating with existing systems and adapting to changing business needs. We differentiate ourselves through our unique approach to problem-solving and commitment to delivering perfect solutions that are easy for clients to understand.

Our Team

We are a focused team of two systems engineering students with extensive project experience in professional-grade applications. Working 100% remotely from Bogotá, Colombia, we leverage modern cloud technologies including Google Cloud, AWS, and Azure to deliver scalable solutions. Our diverse academic background and hands-on project experience enable us to tackle complex technical challenges with innovative approaches.

Our Purpose

Build a respectful, safe and inclusive work environment where technical excellence and creativity drive innovation at great speed. We accompany companies throughout the entire lifecycle of their data and AI products: from strategy and use case discovery, to architecture design, implementation, continuous deployment and business impact measurement.
